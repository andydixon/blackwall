Recommended defaults
Public clients

Use for browser and native apps. They should rely on PKCE and must not depend on a shared client secret.

Confidential clients

Use for server-side services that can store credentials safely and exchange tokens directly.

JWT access tokens

Preferred for modern relying parties that validate signatures locally and call `/oauth/userinfo` for claims.

Authentication age

max_age is chosen by the relying party per login request. BlackWall forces a new passkey check when the current authentication is too old and preserves auth_time across refresh-token rotation.
